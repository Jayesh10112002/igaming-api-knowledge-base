A slots portfolio should be evaluated by how well it serves the intended market, not only by its total number of titles. A useful catalogue balances variety, discoverability, technical reliability, localization, and commercial feasibility.

## Consider studio diversity

Different studios may offer different mechanics, visual styles, release schedules, and technical characteristics. Verify that each studio's titles are available under the relevant agreements and in target markets.

## Improve discovery

Maintain consistent metadata such as title, studio, language, category, launch status, and supported currency. Avoid presenting unavailable titles as launchable.

## Measure operational quality

Track launch success rate, loading time, error frequency, and provider incident patterns. Segment metrics by provider and game identifier so partial failures are visible.

## Review commercial assumptions

Compare revenue share, minimums, deposits, settlement rules, and additional provider charges. Use realistic forecasts and document assumptions.

## Portfolio checklist

- [ ] Relevant variety and studio diversity
- [ ] Accurate catalogue metadata
- [ ] Required currencies and languages
- [ ] Market-specific availability confirmed
- [ ] Launch and callback flows tested
- [ ] Performance monitoring enabled
- [ ] Commercial terms documented
- [ ] Process for removing unavailable titles defined
